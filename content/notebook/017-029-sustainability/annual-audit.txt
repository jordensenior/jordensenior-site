*Darkroom photography, practiced responsibly, is a minor environmental activity.*

### The 80% Rule

If your silver recovery rate is >95%, you've addressed approximately 80% of your darkroom's environmental impact.

If your silver recovery rate is 0%, silver is your priority—everything else is secondary.
