## Two-Bath System Overview

```
FIRST BATH (older)          SECOND BATH (fresher)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━          ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Film/prints enter here  →   Ensures complete fixing
Brief fix time              Full fix time
Accumulates silver          Stays relatively clean

When first bath exhausts:
1. Silver recovery from first bath
2. Discard first bath
3. Promote second → first
4. Make fresh second bath
```

## Testing Guide

### Hypo Check Solution (Potassium Iodide)

Add 2–3 drops to small sample of fixer:

| Result | Meaning | Action |
|--------|---------|--------|
| Clear/light yellow | Fresh, low silver | Continue using |
| Slight cloudiness | Moderate silver | Monitor closely |
| Milky precipitate | High silver, near exhaustion | Rotate baths |

### Film Leader Clearing Test

Drop unexposed film leader into fixer, time until clear:

| Clearing Time | Fixer Status | Action |
|---------------|--------------|--------|
| < 2× normal | Good | Continue using |
| 2–3× normal | Marginal | Test more frequently |
| > 3× normal | Exhausted | Rotate baths |

*Normal clearing time for rapid fixer: 30–60 seconds*

### Silver Test Strips

Follow manufacturer instructions. Generally:
- Compare to colour chart
- Note silver concentration in mg/L
- Rotate when first bath exceeds ~5,000 mg/L

## Typical Capacity (Rapid Fixer)

These are estimates—always verify by testing.

| Material | First Bath | Second Bath | Total System |
|----------|------------|-------------|--------------|
| 35mm film (36 exp) | 20–30 rolls | 40–60 rolls | 60–90 rolls |
| 120 film | 15–20 rolls | 30–40 rolls | 45–60 rolls |
| 8×10 RC paper | 40–60 prints | 80–120 prints | 120–180 prints |
| 8×10 FB paper | 30–40 prints | 60–80 prints | 90–120 prints |

*Higher silver content materials (fast films, FB paper) exhaust fixer faster*

---

## Fix Times (Rapid Fixer)

| Material | First Bath | Second Bath |
|----------|------------|-------------|
| Most B&W films | 30–60 sec | 2–4 min |
| T-grain films (T-Max, Delta) | 60 sec | 3–5 min |
| RC paper | 30 sec | 1 min |
| FB paper | 30–60 sec | 2–3 min |

*First bath time is brief—just to begin the process. Second bath does complete fixing.*
