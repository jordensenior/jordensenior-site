# Series Overview

The notebook documents experimental darkroom practice, alternative photographic processes, and the intersection of analogue chemistry with contemporary science. Each series explores a specific technique, project, or conceptual investigation.

---

## Core Techniques

### [001 · A Brief History of Mordençage](/notebook/001-mordencage/001-mordancage/)
**1 post · August 2025**

Chemistry bites photography. An introduction to mordençage—the process that physically lifts photographic emulsion from its paper base. Covers history (Liesegang to Sudre), chemistry (copper chloride oxidation cycles), modern materials, and safety protocols.

**Start here if:** You want to understand the foundational technique that appears throughout this practice.

---

### [010 · Sabattier in the City of Lights](/notebook/010-sabattier/010-sabattierinc ityoflights/)
**1 post · October 2025**

Partial tone reversal through mid-development exposure. Documents applying the Sabattier effect to Paris street photography using dual-enlarger technique for precise control. Explores the chemistry of silver halide reversal and the Mackie line effect.

**Start here if:** You're interested in controlled darkroom interventions and travel photography.

---

### [011 · Kinetic Darkroom](/notebook/011-kineticdarkroom/011-kineticdarkroom/)
**1 post**

Movement-based interventions during development. Experimental approaches to physical manipulation of prints while chemistry is active.

---

### [017-019 · Lith Printing](/notebook/017-019-lith/)
**3 posts · *Coming soon***

Deep dive into infectious development. Chemistry, control variables, and material selection for lith printing. Currently in development.

## Projects & Series

### [004 · Quantum Mordençage](/notebook/004-quantummordencagezine/004-quantummordencage/)
**1 post · August 2025**

Photographing quantum computing hardware, then chemically disrupting those photographs with mordençage. A conceptual investigation into decoherence, measurement, and uncertainty. Published as a limited-edition Risograph zine (15 copies) and exhibited at Experimental 2025, Barcelona.

**Start here if:** You're interested in the intersection of quantum physics and photographic chemistry.

---

Consumer spaces through mordençage. A multi-part series documenting the creation, exhibition, and re-exhibition of work exploring abandoned commercial environments. Includes installation documentation from Art for All Festival (Turku) and Kaapeli Gallery (Helsinki).
