A simple calculator for determining CMY filtration values for RA4 colour printing. Upload a reference image of your negative, select your film base and paper stock, then adjust the sliders to calculate your starting filtration values.

This tool provides baseline recommendations - always make test strips to verify filtration for your specific setup, as enlarger light source, paper age, and chemistry conditions will affect results.
