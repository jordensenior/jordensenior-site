# Exhibited Works

A collection of photographic works, mordançage experiments, and mixed-media installations that have been exhibited in galleries, festivals, and alternative spaces.

## Beneath the Surface Series (2024-2025)

**Art for All Festival** · *Easton Mall, Columbus, OH* · August 2024

An exploration of consumer spaces through mordançage photography, exhibited in the abandoned Easton Mall during the Art for All festival. The work examined the tension between commercial aspiration and economic reality through chemically manipulated photographic prints.

## About the Work

My photographic practice centers on mordançage, a 19th-century alternative process that allows physical manipulation of photographic emulsion. This technique serves as both material exploration and conceptual framework, investigating themes of transformation, vulnerability, and the intersection of chemistry and chance in image-making.

The work often engages with spaces in transition - abandoned malls, alternative galleries, underground venues - using the physical transformation of the photographic surface to mirror broader questions about impermanence and change in contemporary culture.
