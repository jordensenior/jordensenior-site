# Mordançage Experiments

**2024** · *Foundational Research Series*  
**Medium:** Mordançage on silver gelatin prints  
**Status:** Foundation for exhibition work

Initial explorations of the mordançage process that established the technical and conceptual framework for later exhibition works. These experiments investigate the physical transformation of photographic emulsion and its potential for expressing themes of vulnerability and change.

---

## Process Development

This body of work represents the foundational research phase of my mordançage practice. Through systematic experimentation with different papers, chemicals, and manipulation techniques, I developed the methods later employed in exhibition works like [Beneath the Surface](/works/beneath-the-surface/).

### Technical Discoveries

The mordançage process involves treating developed silver gelatin prints with a bleaching solution that softens the photographic emulsion, allowing it to be physically manipulated. Key technical insights from this experimental phase include:

- **Paper selection:** Different fiber papers respond uniquely to mordançage treatment
- **Timing variations:** Emulsion manipulation windows vary with temperature and solution strength  
- **Surface effects:** Various manipulation techniques create distinct textural qualities
- **Drying considerations:** Different drying methods affect final surface characteristics

## Conceptual Framework

Beyond technical mastery, these experiments established the conceptual foundation for using mordançage as a metaphor for transformation and vulnerability. The process became a way to explore:

**Physical Vulnerability:** The photographic surface becomes literally fragile and manipulable  
**Temporal Transformation:** Images change through time and chemical action  
**Chance and Control:** The balance between intentional manipulation and unpredictable chemical reactions  
**Material Honesty:** Embracing the physical properties of photographic materials

---

## Documentation and Process

This experimental phase was extensively documented to establish reproducible methods for later exhibition work. The systematic approach allowed for consistent results when working under the time pressure of exhibition deadlines.

The learnings from these experiments directly informed:
- Production methods for [Beneath the Surface](/works/beneath-the-surface/)
- Process documentation in [Quantum Mordançage Zine](/works/quantum-mordencage-zine/)
- Technical approaches for future exhibition work

---

## Significance in Practice

While these experiments may appear as individual studies, they function as the foundation for my entire mordançage-based practice. Each technical discovery and conceptual insight developed here enabled the rapid, confident execution of later exhibition work.

This foundational research phase demonstrates the importance of sustained experimental work in developing both technical fluency and conceptual clarity before moving to public presentation.
