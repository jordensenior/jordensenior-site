# Quantum Mordançage Zine

**2024** · *Limited Edition Artist's Zine*  
**Medium:** Risograph printing, hand-folded  
**Edition:** Limited run, ongoing distribution  
**Dimensions:** Standard zine format

A limited-edition artist's zine exploring the intersection of quantum mechanics and photographic chemistry through mordançage processes. Each copy is hand-folded and distributed through alternative art networks and direct engagement.

---

## Concept and Development

The zine emerged from conceptual connections between quantum mechanical principles and the mordançage photographic process. Both involve states of transformation, uncertainty, and the observer's role in determining outcomes. The publication serves as both documentation and theoretical exploration of these parallels.

### Thematic Connections

**Quantum Superposition ↔ Photographic Latent Image:** Both exist in states of potential until observation/development

**Wave-Particle Duality ↔ Mordançage Transformation:** Matter existing in multiple states simultaneously

**Observer Effect ↔ Chemical Manipulation:** The act of observation/interaction fundamentally changes the system

---

## Content and Structure

The zine combines theoretical text, process documentation, and visual examples to create a comprehensive exploration of the quantum-photographic parallels. Content includes:

- Theoretical framework connecting quantum mechanics and photography
- Technical documentation of mordançage processes
- Visual examples showing transformation states
- Conceptual diagrams illustrating quantum-photographic analogies
