## Intersection of Thoughts
**2025** · *Lith Print with Infectious Development*
<br>

<br>
<div class="work-preview">
<img src="/works/intersection-of-thoughts.jpeg" alt="Intersection of Thoughts" class="preview-image" />
<br>

A photographic interpretation of a sculptural installation encountered in Salt Lake City, transformed through lith printing and infectious development techniques. The directional signs—"LESS IS MORE," "HIGH ROAD," overlaying "YOU ARE HERE"—become a meditation on choice and presence. The lith process amplifies the philosophical tension inherent in the original work, using infectious development to push contrast and create the distinctive warm blacks that emphasize the duality between clarity and confusion in navigating life's intersections.
<br>
<br>

**Exhibition:** "Lost and Found" group exhibition, Dark Peak Festival, UK. February 2025. 
</div>
<br>

## About the Work

My photographic practice centers on alternative processes, particularly mordançage, lith, and sabattier techniques, using physical transformation of the photographic surface to explore themes of impermanence and change. The work often engages with spaces in transition - abandoned malls, alternative galleries, underground venues.

For inquiries about exhibitions, collaborations, or acquiring work, please [contact me](mailto:contact@jordensenior.com).
